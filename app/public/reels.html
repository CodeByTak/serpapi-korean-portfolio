Sample campaign · Instagram Reels

How I'd reach Korean developers and sellers on Reels

Three finished sample reels, four ad cards and a 4-week posting calendar for a Korean SerpApi campaign. All on-screen text is Korean, so each piece has an English plot summary and translation below it.

ℹ️The presenters (서윤 Seoyun and 도윤 Doyun) are AI-generated characters made with Google Flow (Veo) and Midjourney, and edited by me. On a live account they would carry Instagram's AI label. The screen recording in Reel 1 is my real Korean search agent running on SerpApi.
